# Voice input

Focus an editable text field: a small microphone appears just above the insertion point, steps aside while you type and takes no room from the field. Select it, or press Alt+Shift+D, to dictate; the same keys end the dictation. The button shows in text fields and search boxes; in record pickers and number or date fields only the shortcut dictates. While you speak, the last words heard are written in the field itself, in grey at the insertion point, until the final text replaces them: every two seconds or so Odoo sends the last twenty seconds for recognition, one request at a time. A dot follows your voice and a timer counts the recording. Select the red stop button to insert the transcript at the selection. Fields that close on an outside click, such as the kanban quick create or a new list line, stay open while you dictate. If you switch fields or edit the destination, Odoo offers the transcript to copy.

**A finished recording is never lost by accident.** The moment you stop, the audio is written to this browser (IndexedDB) and stays there until its text is inserted in the field or copied, or you discard it. If the speech service is busy or asleep, the panel says **Waiting for transcription** and offers **Retry transcription**, **Download audio** (`voice-YYYYMMDD-HHMM.mp3`) and **Discard**; a recording the service heard nothing in stays downloadable too. Escape and the cross beside "Transcribing…" never discard a finished recording: Escape then belongs to whatever else is open, and the cross only stops the wait. Pressing Escape or the cross while still recording, and **Discard**, keep the audio for ten seconds with an **Undo** button before it is freed. If the page closes, reloads, crashes, is closed by the phone or you sign out, the next page offers it again at the bottom of the screen (**Unused dictation (10:42)**) with the same three actions; it is shown only to the person who recorded it, in the database it was recorded in, on the same browser (**Later** folds the panel into a small round count that a tap opens again), and not while another tab of that browser is handling it (the tab holds a Web Lock on it; the tabs tell each other of changes). **Retry transcription** there inserts the text at the selection of the field you are in, or offers it for copying when you are in none. When a recording could not be written to the browser (private browsing, no storage), the browser asks before the page unloads. The audio goes to the speech service for recognition only and is not stored on the server.

In a record’s chatter, choose **Record a voice note** (the waveform icon; the microphone is for dictating text), speak for up to five minutes and select the red stop button to listen to the recording. The last thirty seconds count down. Send the message or log the internal note when ready. A voice note is kept in this browser too, until the message that carries it is posted or you remove the attachment: before that the server holds it only as a pending attachment that Odoo deletes a day later, and a reload loses the composer. If you close the composer or reload before sending, the next page offers the recording (**Attach to the record** uploads it to the record without a composer). If the upload fails, the composer shows **Voice note not uploaded** with **Retry upload**, **Download audio** and **Discard**. If you change record or close the composer while recording, the recording is stopped and uploaded to the record it was made for, as an attachment, rather than dropped; when that fails it is offered again at the start of the next page. Recipients can play the recording while it is transcribed. A person with access can read its text under **Transcript** in the chatter or request the message’s `usl_voice_notes` field. The service receives audio only; the surrounding record and message are never sent.

The application sends MP3 audio to `POST /v1/audio/transcriptions` on the existing internal Ollama gateway. Odoo defaults to `http://ollama:11434`. Set `USL_VOICE_GATEWAY` on Odoo workers to use another internal gateway. The gateway owns worker selection, model readiness and speech recognition. A worker hears one recording at a time and answers 503 with `Retry-After` to the rest. At the end of a dictation the browser, not an Odoo worker, waits: it sends the recording again after the longer of the pause the worker asks for and a doubling pause (2, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 seconds, so at most 7 uploads), for up to 90 seconds, then offers **Retry transcription** with the recording kept (the request carries `client_retry`, which Odoo reads and never forwards; an older page without it still gets a 20 second wait in Odoo). A voice note waits 90 seconds in the queue job. Captions shown while speaking are not sent again, and neither is a 503 without `Retry-After` (a failed recognition, or no worker awake). Odoo sends the recording alone and reads the `language` the gateway heard; the gateway refuses any field other than `language`. When that is neither French nor English, Odoo sends the recording once more with `language` set to the speaker's own language if it is one of the two, French otherwise. After enabling the feature, check dictation in both French and English and send one voice note using an ordinary internal account. Voice notes require the existing OCA queue job runner.
