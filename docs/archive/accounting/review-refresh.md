# Refreshing accounting evidence and reviewed declarations

Supplier evidence checks cover supplier bills, credit notes and receipts. Entries generated from employee expenses are handled by the expense receipt check, including its documented receipt waivers. Attaching evidence or posting an entry keeps its usual Odoo meaning.

Refreshing a declaration updates its automated validation results. It preserves the latest recorded, accepted declaration review and the reviewed preparation state. The reviewer decision and its supporting evidence remain unchanged. A later recorded rejection takes precedence over an older acceptance.

When the generated schedule no longer includes a reviewed declaration, it remains available with its reviewed workflow state. Its validation summary asks for review of the schedule change. An accounting manager can supersede the decision through the existing review action; a later schedule refresh can then retire the declaration normally. Filed, paid and archived declarations retain their existing retirement protection.

Current validation warnings describe current data; they do not replace the recorded reviewer judgment. This change does not file a declaration, modify posted entries or grant review authority.
