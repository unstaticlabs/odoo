# Accounting invariants

## Authoritative ledger

Standard Odoo accounting records are the sole authoritative ledger. Operational assistants and custom workflows may prepare or orchestrate records but cannot create a separate accounting truth.

## Legal entity

Every accounting record belongs to one explicit legal company. Brands, projects and analytic dimensions never substitute for company ownership.

## Balanced and attributable entries

Every posted entry is balanced and identifies its journal, date, company, accounts, debit and credit, currency where relevant, origin and supporting evidence.

## Controlled history

Posted accounting is never silently altered or deleted. Corrections preserve the original record and create an attributable, reviewable correction or reversal according to accepted accounting behaviour.

## Chronology and numbering

Journal chronology and entry references remain controlled. Missing, duplicate or non-chronological references are visible and investigated; migration does not silently renumber history.

## Locks

Fiscal and tax locks prevent unauthorized changes to protected periods. Lock changes are restricted, attributable and evidenced.

## Currency

For foreign-currency transactions, transaction currency, company-currency value, exchange rate consequences, residuals and realized or unrealized differences remain consistent and traceable.

## Reconciliation

Payment state is derived from actual accounting relationships. Full, partial and absent reconciliation remain distinct. Zero residual alone does not prove correct reconciliation.

## Tax

Taxes preserve their bases, amounts, tags, accounts, fiscal treatment and reporting consequences. Manually supplied report values and carryovers are explicit and period-specific.

## Evidence

Required source documents remain readable, linked, access-controlled and retrievable for the applicable retention period.

## Auditability

A reviewer can trace statutory and tax report values to contributing journal items, adjustments and evidence.
