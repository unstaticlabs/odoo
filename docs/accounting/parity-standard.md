# Accounting parity standard

## Purpose

Accounting parity is demonstrated when the Community target reproduces the accepted accounting meaning and outputs of the current production environment for representative USL data.

## Required comparison

Compare, by company and period:

- record counts and states;
- debit and credit totals;
- balances by account and partner;
- open receivables and payables;
- payments and reconciliation relationships;
- tax bases, tax amounts, carryovers and external values;
- transaction and company currencies;
- analytic distributions where used;
- assets and deferred items where used;
- attachments and supporting evidence;
- lock behaviour;
- FEC;
- mandatory accounting reports.

## Mandatory reports

Unless explicitly removed from the approved perimeter, parity includes:

- Trial Balance;
- General Ledger;
- Balance Sheet and applicable French variants;
- Profit and Loss and applicable French variants;
- Partner Ledger;
- Aged Receivable and Aged Payable;
- Journal Report and Open Items;
- French Tax Report and CA12-relevant values;
- FEC;
- Bank Reconciliation Report for imported data;
- unrealized currency gains and losses;
- depreciation and deferred reports where used;
- French annual accounts and intermediate management balances where required.

## Semantic parity

Reports are compared for:

- line meaning and hierarchy;
- account and tax-tag mappings;
- date scope and opening balances;
- signs and rounding;
- company and analytic filters;
- draft, posted and unreconciled filters;
- external values and carryovers;
- cross-report calculations;
- drill-down and exports.

## Difference classes

Every difference is classified as:

- presentation difference;
- accepted product improvement;
- source anomaly;
- transfer defect;
- missing capability;
- legal or accounting uncertainty;
- deliberate non-parity.

Unclassified differences block acceptance.

## Acceptance rule

Accounting values agree to the smallest applicable currency unit under the correct source rounding rules. Any exception is individually explained and accepted by the appropriate stakeholder.
