# French Declarations

The Declarations workspace records applicable obligations and keeps calculated ledger values separate from external confirmations.

Supported coverage includes, when applicable:

- VAT and CA12;
- corporate income tax forms 2065, 2571 and 2572;
- simplified statements in the 2033 series;
- conditional forms configured for the company.

Each declaration shows:

- why the obligation applies;
- the fiscal period and deadline;
- readiness and missing information;
- prefilled values and their source;
- review and external filing status;
- payment, refund or credit status;
- evidence and references.

**View Entries** opens the journal items behind a ledger-derived value. A manual or externally confirmed value is visibly labelled and never silently replaces the ledger calculation.
