# Menus and Screens

## Overview

The daily starting point. It summarizes cash, documents, reconciliation, Hygiene, declarations and closing readiness. Each actionable count opens the relevant records.

## Journals

The native journal dashboard. Use journal cards to open entries, documents, full-width bank transactions or Bank Matching.

## Customers and Vendors

Customer invoices, credit notes, payments, supplier bills, refunds and vendor payments.

## Accounting

Journal entries, journal items, payments, assets, general reconciliation and closing workspaces.

## Review

Accounting Hygiene and other focused review work. Technical audit tools are available only in the advanced area.

## Reporting

The canonical interactive financial, partner, tax, asset, management and analytical reports.

## Declarations

French obligations, periods, deadlines, calculated fields, external confirmations, review and filing status.

## Configuration

Accounting settings, journals, accounts, taxes, analytic dimensions and Closing Controls. Access depends on role.
