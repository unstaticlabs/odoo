# VAT and Declarations

Taxes on business-document lines generate tax bases, tax amounts and tax tags in the ledger. The French VAT report groups those tagged entries into statutory lines for the chosen period.

Depending on tax configuration, exigibility can occur on invoice or payment. Reverse charge, intra-EU services, deductible VAT, asset VAT and zero-rated or exempt revenue therefore depend on both the tax used and the document workflow.

The declaration workspace prepares an operational filing record around those ledger values. It distinguishes:

- calculated ledger values;
- explicit external adjustments or confirmations;
- review status;
- filing and settlement status.

Use **View Entries** before changing or confirming a value. An external value supplements the traceable calculation; it does not silently overwrite it.
