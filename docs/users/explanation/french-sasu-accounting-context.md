# French SASU Accounting Context

Audience: Valentin, accountant, finance operator.

Unstatic Labs is treated as a French SASU accounting company. The rebuilt accounting product is designed around the company's actual review needs, not a generic demo company.

## Main Company Boundary

Unstatic Labs and USL Media are separate accounting companies.

A brand, project, product or analytic account is not a legal company.

This matters because statutory reports, tax reports, FEC files, journals, taxes and lock dates must be company-specific.

## Benchmark Period

The primary benchmark period is:

```text
2024-01-10 to 2025-09-30
```

For this period, the system focuses on preserving posted accounting history and producing accountant-reviewable reports and exports.

## French Outputs

The implemented user-facing evidence includes:

- French annual statements;
- Bilan and Compte de resultat evidence;
- SIG and CAF evidence;
- French tax-package mapping;
- VAT and CA12 review values;
- FEC export;
- fixed-asset and depreciation evidence.

## What the System Does Not Do

The system does not:

- submit tax declarations automatically;
- provide accountant attestation;
- replace professional tax judgment;
- decide reduced-rate corporate tax eligibility by itself;
- merge personal and company accounting;
- turn USL Media into Unstatic Labs;
- copy Odoo Enterprise report code.

It provides structured accounting evidence so the accountant and CEO can review, decide and export.
