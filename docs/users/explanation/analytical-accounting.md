# Analytical Accounting

General accounting answers which legal account was affected. Analytical accounting explains which activity, brand, project or other management dimension caused it.

A document line may distribute its amount across one or more analytic plans. Posting creates analytic items connected to the journal items, so reports can be filtered and drilled back to source documents.

**Revenue vs Spending** keeps revenue and spending as components and shows net contribution separately. Net contribution is revenue minus spending; it is not a third stacked source of value.

Analytic totals should reconcile to the selected general-ledger scope. Missing distributions are review work surfaced by Accounting Hygiene.
