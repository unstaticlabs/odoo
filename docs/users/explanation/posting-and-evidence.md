# Posting and Evidence

A draft invoice, bill, expense or entry is preparation work. Posting turns it into journal items and makes it part of the legal and management ledgers.

Posted documents should be corrected through the accounting workflow—such as a credit note, reversal or permitted reset—not by silently changing their economic meaning.

Evidence explains why a transaction exists: supplier invoices, receipts, contracts, payment confirmations and review notes. Attach it to the business document whenever possible so the path remains:

> evidence → business document → journal entry → reconciliation → report

Accounting Hygiene highlights missing or incomplete preparation without posting on the user’s behalf.

Evidence restored from Odoo Online appears on the same native record and, when
it came from a source message, in an internal chatter note. Opening or
downloading it uses the normal Odoo attachment controls; there is no separate
migration screen.

An uploaded supplier document that had not yet been extracted in the source is
kept as an empty draft bill with its file. It has no ledger effect until a
Finance user reviews the document, completes the bill and posts it.

Accounting Managers and the scoped read-only accountant can open evidence only
when they can read its parent Accounting record and company. The read-only role
cannot post new chatter, upload, replace, delete or relink the file. Access to
Accounting evidence never grants access to unrelated private or technical
attachments.
