# Closing and Lock Dates

Closing is a controlled review of a period, not a single report or button.

A Closing Workspace brings together unresolved issues, reconciliations, reports,
declarations, FEC availability, evidence and lock dates. Enabled Accounting
Controls determine what passes, informs, warns or blocks. The same company
catalogue powers daily Hygiene; a definition can apply to one workflow or both.

The Overview links to the most recently ended annual workspace still open for review. Its figures count entries posted within that financial year, the journals used by those entries and how many of those journals are now archived. The current closing workspace can cover a more recent month or quarter. Open either workspace by name to inspect its dates.

The configured impact controls readiness. **Dynamic** keeps the evaluator's
contextual result; **Informational** remains visible; **Advisory** creates a
warning; **Blocking** prevents readiness. A technical failure is shown
separately from an accounting exception and blocks a false Ready conclusion
until the evaluator works again.

Preparation does not close the accounts. Internal approval records that the configured blocking work is complete. Lock dates then protect journal entries for the relevant population and period according to the user’s authority.

The company’s configured recurring fiscal-year end and exceptional first-year
boundaries drive the workspace and declaration periods. The same boundaries
also drive reports, FEC defaults, cash/tax projections, analytics and
accounting spreadsheets. Accountant review can be recorded, but its absence
does not prevent the internal product from preparing a complete, reproducible
closing package.

## Reading control results

**Export available** means an accountant can open the reporting or FEC tool. Review opens it for the closing company and dates. Compare the generated files with the books before accepting their content. FEC opens in test mode. Its download keeps the closing company, including when another company is selected in the browser. The readiness summary shows how many successful checks establish only export availability. In **Accepted Snapshots**, open the actual package file and its recorded review decision to see which files a reviewer accepted.

**Document completeness** counts draft invoices and credit notes and posted documents without a main attachment. Review opens the affected documents. A supporting file may already exist in Documents without being linked as the main attachment.

**Accounting Hygiene** in a close counts open actionable findings through the end date, including undated findings. The company overview includes informational findings and all dates. Its operational reminders also cover banking, missing attachments, outstanding declarations and decisions; one item can prompt several reminders.
