# Closing and Lock Dates

Closing is a controlled review of a period, not a single report or button.

A Closing Workspace brings together unresolved issues, reconciliations, reports,
declarations, FEC availability, evidence and lock dates. Enabled Accounting
Controls determine what passes, informs, warns or blocks. The same company
catalogue powers daily Hygiene; a definition can apply to one workflow or both.

The configured impact controls readiness. **Dynamic** keeps the evaluator's
contextual result; **Informational** remains visible; **Advisory** creates a
warning; **Blocking** prevents readiness. A technical failure is shown
separately from an accounting exception and blocks a false Ready conclusion
until the evaluator works again.

Preparation does not close the accounts. Internal approval records that the configured blocking work is complete. Lock dates then protect journal entries for the relevant population and period according to the user’s authority.

The company’s configured recurring fiscal-year end and exceptional first-year
boundaries drive the workspace and declaration periods. The same boundaries
also drive reports, FEC defaults, cash/tax projections, analytics and
accounting spreadsheets. Accountant review can be recorded, but its absence
does not prevent the internal product from preparing a complete, reproducible
closing package.
