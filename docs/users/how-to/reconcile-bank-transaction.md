# Match a Bank Transaction

1. Open **Journals** and locate the bank journal.
2. If the transactions are not present yet, choose **Import Statement** on the journal card. Upload a CAMT, QIF, CSV or XLSX file, then choose **Import and View**. CSV/XLSX files use the mapping configured on that journal.
3. Return to the journal card and select **Bank Matching**.
4. Select a transaction from the queue.
5. In **Reconcile**, review the closest opposite-sign amounts first. The removable **Closest amount** and **Closest date** chips are defaults, not restrictions.
6. Choose an existing invoice, bill, payment or journal item when it is the true counterpart.
7. For a direct charge or receipt, deliberately choose a manual operation, then set partner, account, tax and label.
8. Check the remaining difference before confirming.
9. After matching, keep the result visible. Confirm the matching-reference chip, Full or Partial status, and residual.
10. Use **Undo** if the counterpart is wrong. For a partial match, continue with the remaining residual later.

**Transactions** is the full-width bank history and investigation screen. **Bank Matching** is the operational queue. They are intentionally separate.
