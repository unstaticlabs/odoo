# Send product feedback

Use Feedback for a bug, improvement, product question or UX problem without
leaving the page you are working on.

1. Open the messages icon at the top right and choose **Chats**.
2. Select **Feedback**. Your browser asks which screen or tab to capture.
3. Choose the current Odoo screen. The captured image is attached by default;
   clear **Attach this screenshot** if you do not want to share it. Cancelling
   capture does not block feedback.
4. Describe what you were trying to do, what happened and what you expected.
   Add other evidence if useful.
5. Leave **Share sanitized page context** off unless the current Odoo record is
   useful. When enabled, Odoo shares only a readable action/model/record,
   company, viewport and exact release—not the URL, query, fragment, tokens,
   browser state or form values.
6. Select **Start conversation**. Odoo saves an Inbox card before contacting
   the Feedback Assistant.
7. Answer any short clarification questions in the native conversation. You
   can close the drawer or reload Odoo and continue the card from **Chats >
   Feedback** later.
8. Review the assistant's proposed summary and brief. Choose **Confirm and send
   to Triage** only when it accurately represents your feedback.

The shared **Board** action opens all product feedback for internal employees.
Everyone may read and discuss cards and attachments; only maintainers may edit
their fields or workflow stage. Your source company is retained for context,
but the shared board does not reveal source business records that another
employee cannot read.

If the assistant is unavailable, your card remains saved in Inbox and shows a
safe retry action. If the page record became inaccessible, Odoo omits that
record and tells you. Screenshot and file size errors are shown before the
conversation starts. Provider processing is external to Odoo and subject to
the retention disclosure shown in the drawer; task status and chatter
notifications stay inside Odoo rather than being emailed.
