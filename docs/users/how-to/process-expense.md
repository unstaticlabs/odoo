# Process an Expense

1. Open **Vendors > Expenses** and select **New**, or submit a receipt through the configured expense intake.
2. Choose the employee, date, category, paid-by method and currency.
3. Attach the receipt.
4. Check taxes and analytic distribution.
5. Submit the expense and complete the configured approval.
6. Post it to accounting.
7. If the employee paid, reimburse the payable. If the company paid, keep the company-payment link.
8. Match the related bank transaction.
9. Inspect the journal entry and the Revenue vs Spending or analytic report.

If Accounting Hygiene reports a stale expense or missing receipt, use **Open Source Record** to continue exactly this workflow.
