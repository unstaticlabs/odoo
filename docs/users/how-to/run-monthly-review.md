# Run a Monthly Review

1. Open **Overview** and clear urgent document and bank queues.
2. Open **Bank Statements**. For each expected bank month, resolve missing or
   failed exports, confirm that the official PDF is available in Documents,
   compare its opening and closing balances, and certify only when continuity
   and movement-population checks pass. A missing PDF blocks certification but
   does not undo correctly imported OFX transactions.
3. Review Accounting Hygiene and resolve or document focused issues.
4. Reconcile bank, receivable, payable, suspense and clearing accounts.
5. Review Balance générale, Grand livre, Grand livre auxiliaire, ageing, Bilan,
   Compte de résultat, TVA et taxes and Compte de résultat analytique.
6. Check assets, depreciation, deferrals, foreign currency and analytics.
7. Open the month’s **Closing Workspace** and select **Refresh Readiness**.
8. Open each Warning or Blocking control and follow its next action.
9. Re-run reports and retain PDF/XLSX evidence when needed.
10. Review applicable declarations.
11. Select **Prepare Review**. When blockers are clear, approve internally or request optional accountant review.

Do not apply lock dates until you intend to protect the reviewed period.
