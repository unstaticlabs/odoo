# Provide Read-Only Accountant Access

Assign the scoped **Accountant Reviewer** role and only the intended companies.

The reviewer can inspect documents, journal items, reconciliations, declarations, closing material and interactive reports; filter and drill down; download PDF/XLSX; and generate a complete test FEC.

The reviewer cannot create or edit accounting, post, reconcile, undo, configure, close, apply lock dates or generate the final FEC path.

Before handing over access:

1. switch to the reviewer account;
2. confirm the visible company set;
3. open documents, General Reconciliation, reports, declarations and FEC;
4. confirm mutation buttons are hidden;
5. attempt no real accounting change merely to test a denial—use the maintained role tests instead.
