# Reconcile a Clearing or Other Account

1. Open **Accounting > General Reconciliation**.
2. Select **Unreconciled**.
3. Filter the account, partner and period; group when useful.
4. Select debit and credit lines that belong together.
5. Confirm whether they balance fully. If not, reconcile partially only when the residual is real and understood.
6. Reconcile and keep the affected lines on screen.
7. Read the colored matching-reference chip, Full or Partial status and remaining residual.
8. Open the source documents if the match is unclear.
9. Use **Undo** from the reconciled result when necessary.

Use **Reconciled** to audit existing matches and **All** to investigate both open and matched lines together.
