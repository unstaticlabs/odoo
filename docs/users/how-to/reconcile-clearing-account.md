# Reconcile a Clearing or Other Account

1. Open **Accounting > General Reconciliation**.
2. Keep the active **Unreconciled** filter, then expand the account you want to clear.
3. Filter the partner and period when useful.
4. Choose **Find counterpart** on the first debit or credit line.
5. In **Available counterparts**, select the opposite-sign line or lines that belong with it. **Closest amount** is an active, removable sorting aid; it does not make the decision for you.
6. Check the selected debit, credit and remaining difference above the table. The page says **Balanced selection** for a full match and **Partial match** when a residual will remain.
7. Select **Confirm match**. It is available only when at least one debit and one credit from the same account are selected.
8. Keep the result on screen and read the colored matching-reference chip, Full or Partial status and remaining residual.
9. For a partial match, use **Continue matching** to settle the residual later. Use **Undo** to remove the reconciliation links without deleting the accounting entries.

Use **Reconciled** to audit existing matches and **All** to investigate both open and matched lines together.
