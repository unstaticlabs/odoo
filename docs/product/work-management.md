# Work management

## Purpose

Odoo coordinates business meaning, responsibility, dependencies, decisions and completion evidence. GitHub remains authoritative for code and pull requests.

## Work item standard

An execution-ready work item contains:

- objective and business context;
- accountable owner;
- current state;
- expected deliverable;
- acceptance criteria;
- dependencies and waiting conditions;
- relevant evidence and history;
- authority and escalation rules.

## State rules

- “In progress” means active work is occurring.
- “Waiting” identifies an external event or dependency.
- “Blocked” identifies a condition preventing progress and its escalation path.
- “Pending approval” identifies the decision and approver.
- “Done” means the accepted real-world outcome and evidence exist.

An attempted action is not completion.

## Human-agent collaboration

Agents collaborate primarily through durable shared state. Human activities represent decisions or genuine blockers, not routine execution.

A conversation, email or voice note may become a work item only after relevant context, ownership and acceptance expectations have been added.

## Signal quality

Projects must distinguish strategy, current execution, waiting work, research, future ideas and archive. Urgency must be exceptional and justified.
