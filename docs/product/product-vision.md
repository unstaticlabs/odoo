# Product vision

## Mandate

The USL Odoo Distribution is the structured operating core of the USL
Automated Organization.

It must let a small number of humans and specialized agents operate with reliable shared memory, clear responsibility and auditable execution. Humans provide vision, judgment, creativity, empathy, approval and accountability. Agents progressively perform repeatable operational work within bounded authority.

## Odoo's role

Odoo is the principal source of truth for structured business and accounting reality:

- legal entities, partners and responsibilities;
- accounting records and financial consequences;
- projects, work items, decisions and operating state;
- policies, evidence, permissions and audit history;
- metrics, warnings and required interventions.

Odoo does not need to own every source artefact. It must own the structured business interpretation that makes external events operationally meaningful.

## Business outcome

The Distribution has replaced the Odoo Online capabilities USL uses. It must
preserve that production reality while remaining a better foundation for:

- French SME accounting;
- multi-company operations;
- reliable project execution;
- custom workflows;
- accountant collaboration;
- bounded AI-agent participation;
- future products such as Yoshi.

## Invariants

- Legal entities, brands, projects and analytic dimensions remain distinct.
- Standard business and accounting reality remains authoritative.
- Posted accounting history is controlled and traceable.
- Sensitive actions require authority defined by policy.
- Failed automation leaves visible, actionable state.
- Work is not complete until the required real-world state exists.
- Production changes require demonstrated integrity, recoverability and
  explicit admission.
