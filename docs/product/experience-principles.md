# Experience principles

## Prepared decisions

Human attention is reserved for decisions, approvals, signatures, genuine ambiguity and sensitive situations.

A high-quality request to a human explains:

- what happened;
- why it matters;
- the recommended action;
- the consequence of approval;
- the consequence of inaction;
- the supporting evidence.

Activities must not become a queue of work that an authorized agent can complete itself.

## Opinionated simplicity

The system should infer or establish sensible policies rather than expose unnecessary configuration choices. Important evidence and consequences appear first; deeper detail remains available through progressive disclosure.

### Focused application launcher

The Distribution launcher emphasizes the operational products USL expects
people to enter directly. Discussion, To-do, generic Dashboards, Apps, Link
Tracker and Official Documents remain installed for framework compatibility
and may still be reached from an intentional workflow, but they are not
launcher destinations. This applies to administrators and debug mode as well
as ordinary internal users; technical access must not silently turn the
launcher back into a module catalogue.

## Sparse signals

Chatter, urgency, warnings and notifications must remain high-signal. Success messages should not create work. Warnings must identify the affected object, consequence and next action.

## Clear state

Draft, active, waiting, blocked, pending approval, completed and cancelled are distinct meanings.

Waiting for an external party is not completion. A blocked item identifies what it is waiting for, expected evidence, recognition criteria, downstream consequences and escalation conditions.

## Reversibility and safety

Safe review and correction paths must exist wherever legally and operationally possible. Before approval, the user must understand what will happen.

## Direct and conversational access

Odoo remains directly usable, but routine capture and review may occur through mobile, voice, Telegram, ChatGPT or other approved surfaces. Conversational convenience must not weaken structure, permissions or auditability.

## Privacy

Access to accounting evidence or an operational object does not imply access to unrelated private material.
