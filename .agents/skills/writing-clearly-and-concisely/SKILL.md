# Write clearly and concisely

- Lead with the result or required action.
- Use active voice and concrete terms.
- Keep one idea per paragraph and related words together.
- Remove repetition, filler, puffery, unnecessary headings, and excessive
  formatting.
- Preserve required facts, decisions, caveats, evidence, and next steps.
- Prefer the shortest wording that remains precise and natural in context.
